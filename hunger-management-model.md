## The Three Domains

Understanding where your hunger originates is the key to managing it effectively.

### 1. Biological Signals
*Evolution-shaped signals that regulate survival*

- **Ghrelin** — Pulses tied to circadian rhythm; rises before meals, falls after eating.
- **Satiety Hormones** — GLP-1, PYY, and CCK signal fullness to the brain.
- **Energy-Status Cues** — Insulin, leptin, and hepatic glycogen reflect stored energy.
- **Autonomic Tone** — Sympathetic vs parasympathetic balance regulates digestion.

**Cycle:** Hunger → Eating → Satiety → Quiet Interval → (repeat)

### 2. Cue-Driven Signals
Conditioned environmental and psychological triggers that activate eating behavior independent of true biological need.

### 3. Structural Forces
Systemic and environmental factors that shape food availability, marketing, and consumption patterns.
